# Why I Want to Be a SOC Analyst

I want to become a SOC Analyst because I am interested in cybersecurity and want to understand how organizations protect their systems, networks, and data from cyber threats.

As a Cybersecurity student, I want to build my career around practical security skills rather than only learning theory. A SOC Analyst role interests me because it involves monitoring security alerts, analyzing logs, investigating suspicious activities, and helping organizations respond to security incidents.

I also like the fact that cybersecurity is a field where continuous learning is important. New threats, vulnerabilities, and attack techniques appear regularly, so there is always something new to learn. I want to improve my knowledge of networking, Linux, Windows, Python, SIEM tools, and security monitoring.

My goal is to start from the fundamentals and gradually develop the skills required for a real SOC environment. I plan to practice through platforms such as TryHackMe, LetsDefend, Microsoft Learn, Splunk, Hack The Box, and CyberDefenders.

I know becoming a good SOC Analyst will take time and consistent practice. I may make mistakes while learning, but I want to document my progress, build projects, and improve step by step.

My long-term goal is to become a skilled cybersecurity professional who can confidently investigate and respond to real-world security incidents.
